# Hooks, openers, and first seconds (short-form vertical)

Use this file as **grounding context** for punchy on-screen lines and cold opens. Paraphrased from common creator guidance; test what works for your niche.

## Why the opening matters

- Viewers often decide **very quickly** whether to keep watching or scroll.
- A weak first beat can limit how far a clip is shown, even if the middle and end are strong.
- **Sound-off viewing** is common on phones—**large, high-contrast on-screen text** (short phrases) helps.

Sources: [Virvid — hooks and pattern interrupts](https://virvid.ai/blog/hooks-pattern-interrupts-viral-openers-in-2026), [Virvid — first 3 seconds](https://virvid.ai/blog/first-3-seconds-hook-faceless-shorts-2026), [Postigniter — hook formulas](https://postigniter.com/blog/high-performance-hooks-writing-scroll-stopping-intros-for-short-form-video)

## Hook patterns that appear often in guides

1. **Bold claim** — one controversial or surprising sentence.
2. **Curiosity gap** — “The reason your …” / “What nobody tells you about …”
3. **Micro-story** — one line of story before the lesson.
4. **Visual or motion interrupt** — cut, zoom, prop, or text pop in the first second.
5. **Direct question** — speaks straight to the viewer’s problem.

## Execution tips (script + edit)

- **Layer cues:** Combine **visual + text + spoken line** when possible in the first 1–2 seconds.
- **Pacing:** Frequent **scene or text changes** (roughly every few seconds) can help hold attention on fast feeds—adjust for your brand.
- **Text on screen:** Prefer **5–8 words per line**, large type, high contrast—readable in a vertical frame.
- **Test:** Try multiple hooks for the same core idea; small changes in the first line often change retention the most.

Sources: [Reezo — viral hooks (2025)](https://reezo.ai/blog/viral-video-hooks-that-work-2025), [Hashmeta — vertical templates](https://hashmeta.com/blog/vertical-video-templates-master-tiktok-and-reels-formats-for-maximum-engagement/)

## Lines you can adapt (fill in your niche)

- “Stop scrolling if you [specific outcome].”
- “I wasted [time/money] so you don’t have to.”
- “Three signs you’re [problem]—and what to do instead.”
- “Unpopular opinion: [claim relevant to niche].”
